## 🎯 What It Shows

Why language models guess instead of saying "I don't know" — and why this **remains unsolved**. Four interactive scenes:

1. **The Quiz** — 5 questions, 3 traps about things that don't exist. The model always answers, confidently. Reveals that confidence **does not separate** truth.
2. **The Incentives** — Error-penalty slider. Shows live that `E[guess] = p·1 + (1−p)·(−penalty)` — when the penalty is 0, guessing always wins.
3. **Calibration** — Stated vs actual confidence curve. Typical LLM (ECE ~16%) vs calibrated (ECE ~1%).
4. **Philosophy** — Socrates, Dunning-Kruger, and the open dispute among researchers.
