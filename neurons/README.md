## 🎯 What It Shows

Three different types of artificial neuron solve the same classification problem, live:

| Neuron | Operation | Philosopher |
|---|---|---|
| **Additive** | Weighted sum `Σ wᵢxᵢ` | Aristotle — synthesis |
| **Divisive** | Normalization by division | Plato — diairesis |
| **Hybrid** | 50/50 combination | Leibniz — harmony |

The user trains all three in parallel and watches the decision boundaries, loss curve and accuracy update live.

## 🔬 Scientific Background

The **Divisive Neuron** is not a theoretical construct — it corresponds to **Divisive Normalization**, a real brain mechanism (Carandini & Heeger, 2012), and to the core of Transformer **attention** (`QKᵀ/√d`).
